Hausdorff Metric-Based Kernel Flows for Learning Invariant Sets in Dynamical Systems: A Variational Approach

Jahagirdar, J., Hamzi, B., Owhadi, H., Kevrekidis, Y.

Physica D: Nonlinear Phenomena, 2026

A variational framework in a reproducing kernel Hilbert space that learns invariant sets of dynamical systems directly from time-series data, including saddle-type and non-attracting structures that forward simulation cannot reach. The kernel is learned through a bilevel scheme: inner optimisation on an invariant-set energy, outer kernel update by cross-validation.

Evaluated on 130 in-scope chaotic systems from dysts, d = 3 to 6. 98 of 130 reconstructed with Hausdorff distance below 5.0, median HD 0.323. Degrades gracefully with dimension: median HD 0.079 at d = 3, 0.473 at d ≥ 6. 37 further systems classified as out of scope by structural cause.
